Social Integrations will bring your community's social side onto your Pano website. The plan is to show your social media content, links, widgets and community platforms right on your pages, so visitors can find and follow you without leaving your site. This addon is still being built, and the first version is coming soon.

## ✨ Planned features
- 📣 **Your socials on your site**: Show content from the platforms where your community hangs out.
- 🔗 **Social links**: Point visitors to all your channels from one place.
- 🧩 **Widgets**: Add social and community widgets to your website.
- 💬 **Community platforms**: Bring your community spaces closer to your players.
- 🎨 **Fits your theme**: Made for Pano websites, so it matches the look of your site.

## 🚀 Get started
Social Integrations is coming to the Pano store soon. Once it is there:

1. Open your Pano panel and go to **Addons**.
2. Find **Social Integrations** and click **Install**.
3. Open the addon's settings and connect the platforms you want to show.
